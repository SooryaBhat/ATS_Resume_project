Platform Overview

Comprehensive AI Career Suite

TalentMatch AI bridges the gap between candidates and recruiters by using advanced NLP models to evaluate, optimize, and tailor resumes for maximum ATS compatibility.

📄

Resume Parsing

Extracts deep entity structures, work history, skills, and metrics from PDF resumes using spaCy and custom NLP pipelines.

🎯

ATS Scoring

Computes real-time ATS match percentage scores evaluating formatting, keyword density, and structural compliance.

🔍

JD Matching

Compares resumes against specific Job Descriptions to identify critical missing keywords and skill requirements.

Skill Gap Roadmap

PDF Export Reports
